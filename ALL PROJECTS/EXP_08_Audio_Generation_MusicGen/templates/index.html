Experiment 8: MusicGen Audio LLM Architecture

How Transformer Language Models generate continuous musical audio directly from natural language prompts.

1

T5 Text Encoder

Embeds the text prompt into continuous semantic vectors that guide musical genre, instruments, and mood.

→
2

Autoregressive Decoder

A 300M parameter causal Transformer that predicts the next discrete acoustic tokens at 50 Hz ("autocomplete for sound").

→
3

EnCodec Neural Codec

Decodes multi-stream codebook vectors into continuous 32 kHz high-fidelity stereo audio waveforms.

Codebook Tokenization

EnCodec compresses 32,000 samples per second into 4 discrete parallel codebooks at 50 Hz (200 tokens/sec total), making audio generation as efficient as text generation.

LoRA Fine-Tuning

Low-Rank Adaptation freezes the 300M foundation weights and trains ~100MB adapter matrices on genre datasets (e.g. Free Music Archive) without catastrophic forgetting.
