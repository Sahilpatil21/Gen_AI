Experiment 6: DCGAN Theory & Architecture

Deep Convolutional Generative Adversarial Networks on the CelebA Dataset (by Dr. Sachin Balawant Takmare)

1. Minimax Adversarial Game

The Generator $G$ and Discriminator $D$ are trained simultaneously in a zero-sum competition:

min_G max_D E[log D(x)] + E[log(1 - D(G(z)))]

Over training iterations, $G$ learns to model the true data distribution $p_{data}$ of human faces.

2. Generator Transposed Convolutions

Replaces fully connected layers with fractional-strided convolutions (ConvTranspose2d):

  • Input: z ~ N(0, I) (100-D vector)
  • Project & reshape: 100 -> 512 x 4 x 4
  • Up-conv layers: 4x4 -> 8x8 -> 16x16 -> 32x32 -> 64x64
  • Output activation: Tanh in range [-1, 1]

3. Stabilization Guidelines (Radford et al.)

  • Batch Normalization in both Generator and Discriminator
  • ReLU activation in Generator for all layers except output (Tanh)
  • LeakyReLU (0.2) activation in Discriminator
  • Adam optimizer with $\beta_1 = 0.5$ for momentum stabilization

4. Latent Space Continuity & SLERP

Because the latent space is continuous, interpolating between two latent points $z_A$ and $z_B$ via Spherical Linear Interpolation (SLERP) produces smooth, believable identity transitions without unnatural distortion.
