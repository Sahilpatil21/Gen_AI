Exercise 4: AI Image Generation Architecture

Deep dive into the Text-to-Image diffusion pipeline powering this studio.

Hardware Optimization

To operate seamlessly on systems with 2GB VRAM without encountering CUDA Out-of-Memory (OOM), the engine employs sequential CPU offloading, attention slicing, and FP16 half-precision tensor quantization.

Multi-Engine Acceleration

Users can toggle between local Diffusers inference, ultra-fast cloud diffusion synthesis (sub-3s generation), or procedural canvas fallback.
